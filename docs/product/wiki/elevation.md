# Height

This page owns height. It does not own how crust thickens or thins. That is the tectonics page.

Height is isostasy. It is how far the crust stands above the ocean baseline. Ocean crust is thickness 8, and that thickness stands at height 0. Thicker crust stands higher, one step of height for each step of thickness past 8. Thinner crust stands below the line. The map paints below the line as deeper water. How the crust comes to be that thick is on the [tectonics](tectonics.md) page.

At the start every cell is that baseline. There are no mountains yet. Mountains appear only after the plates have worked, because the height is a reading of the crust and the crust starts as flat ocean.

The person can look at height, at the plates, or at the boundaries. Changing the view does not change the height.
