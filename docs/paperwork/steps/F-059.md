# F-059 — Margin relief

**Goal:** G-010  
**Status:** `done`

## Job (approved)

After new rift ids exist, and before isostasy, write a thickness reaction into oceanic lockers around divergence and collision. A split becomes a trough: thinnest on the contact, back to thickness 8 at distance 8. A collision thickens oceanic neighbors by a falloff that is 0 at distance 4, and this step cannot raise a locker to 16. The outer lip of a split is not a straight band of fresh 8s: a deterministic choice blends some of those cells toward the neighbor, within 4 cells. Pass-by is unchanged. Continental lockers (thickness ≥ 16) are not written. Elevation stays thickness minus 8. Same seed and step count stay identical. No engine edits.

Suture, arc, and cap stay F-060. Goal close stays F-061.

## Decisions (approved)

| Topic | Choice |
|-------|--------|
| When | After `RidgeCreate`, before `ThicknessToElevation` |
| Distance | Orthogonal steps on the sphere grid, from both cells of a post-move contact |
| Diverge radius | 8 |
| Trough | `4 + d/2` for distance `d` in 0..8. Contact (`d = 0`) is 4. Distance 8 is 8 |
| Collide radius | 4. Bonus `4 - d` for `d < 4`, and 0 at distance 4 |
| Collide ceiling | If the locker was below 16, the result is at most 15 |
| Continental | Thickness ≥ 16 is not written |
| Lip | Distance 1..4 from a split. Half the cells, by a seed hash, blend `(trough + neighbor thickness) / 2` toward the farther neighbor. Distance 0 stays on the trough |
| Shared locker | The cell closest to the split decides the trough. The cell closest to the collision decides the bonus |
| Pass-by | No margin write |
| Out of scope | Suture, arc, cap, plate-velocity wobble, locker GC, `engine` |

## Functional requirements (approved)

| ID | Requirement (measurable) | Status |
|----|--------------------------|--------|
| FR-1 | After a diverge, oceanic thickness is lowest on the contact and back to 8 at distance 8. Lockers at thickness 16 or more beside that rift do not change. | met |
| FR-2 | After a collide, oceanic neighbors thicken by a falloff that is 0 at distance 4, and none of those lockers reach 16 from this step alone. | met |
| FR-3 | On a long diverge, the outer lip does not mint as a straight band of 8s. A deterministic choice blends some of those edge cells toward the neighbor, within 4 cells, stable for the same seed. | met |
| FR-4 | Elevation remains thickness minus 8. Same seed and step count give the same occupancy, lockers, and elevation. No engine edits. | met |
