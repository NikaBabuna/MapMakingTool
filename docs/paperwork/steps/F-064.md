# F-064 — Conceptual product shelf

**Goal:** G-011
**Status:** `done`

## Job (approved)

Rewrite `docs/product/` so it explains the product to a person, with no mention of the program. The pages are highly conceptual. How the application is built and how it operates is recorded on the architecture shelf, not here. This Step does not create that shelf and does not edit `architecture.md`; the operational record stays in the papers that already hold it until a later Step gathers them. The door, `concept.md`, `glossary.md`, `style-guide.md`, and the wiki (`world.md`, `tectonics.md`, `elevation.md`) are rewritten. `flows.md` becomes `journeys.md`. Each world rule is stated in full on one page. The current rules stay: the window is 1920×1080 cells, the map joins as a sphere on a rectangle, a seed grows 12 to 24 plates, ocean thickness starts at 8, land is crust at least 16 thick, mountains stop at 32, a rift opens thin ocean, ocean subducts, two continents thicken and do not merge, and elevation is how far the crust stands above the ocean. Explore, Guide, and Timeline are labeled as not built. `docs/project/project.md` stays as it is. No simulation behavior changes. The journey blueprint and the dictionary line that name `docs/product/flows.md` are updated to `journeys.md`, and so are the entrance and the other links to that path. Earlier phrase checks that require Step ids, Goal ids, and type names inside these product pages, or that open `docs/product/flows.md`, are retargeted onto the world fact they were standing in for, and onto `journeys.md`. They are not deleted. Checks that read paperwork, decisions, `architecture.md`, or source stay as they are. No new test is added whose job is to search documents for phrases. The Goal’s F-064 line is updated to this job’s name when the Step is stored.
