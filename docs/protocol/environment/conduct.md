# Conduct

1. Follow [dispatch.md](dispatch.md). Do not invent a write sequence.
2. Store approved requirements in the Step record before implementation.
3. Mark the Step `in progress` before implementation.
4. One Step is `in progress` at a time.
5. Accept only when this Step’s witness holds and every earlier Accepted Step’s witness still holds.
6. Do not delete or soften a requirement or a test to make a witness pass.
7. Do not claim Accept from a status line. The witness Accepts.
8. Domain language is written on the product shelf. Do not leave it only in chat or only in source.
9. A torn Step is rolled back. Failure must not look like success.
10. Touch only the files the approved job and the active flow name.
