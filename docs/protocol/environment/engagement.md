# Engagement

The human approves a Goal, and approves each Step’s job and requirements, before those records are written.

Ask, and wait, before:

- Starting a Step
- Changing stored requirements
- A refactor beyond the approved job
- Expanding scope
- Continuing after a witness keeps failing
- Choosing a package layout the implementation paper does not already name

A reply states, in plain English:

- What changed
- What that means
- What was witnessed
- What remains on the Goal

The shape of the message is the reply blueprint. The sequence of propose, wait, and write is the flow the dispatch names. This page is the standing rule, not the script.
