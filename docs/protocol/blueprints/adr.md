# Decision section

**Kind:** one decision  
**Document:** an `## ADR-0xx` section inside `docs/project/decisions.md` (ADR-001 through ADR-013 exist today)

## Body

1. Date and status.
2. The decision in plain sentences.
3. Why.
4. What it supersedes or amends, when it does.
5. A link to the Goal when it belongs to one.

## Must not

Be its own file.
