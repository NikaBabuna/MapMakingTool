# Decision log

**Kind:** decision log  
**Document:** `docs/project/decisions.md`

## Body

A sequence of `## ADR-0xx` sections. The log introduces what an ADR is in a few lines. New decisions are appended. They are not separate files.

## Must not

Be the paper that states current mechanism. Point at the ADR; the paper states what is true now.
