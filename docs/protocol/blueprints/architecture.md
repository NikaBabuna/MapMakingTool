# Implementation paper

**Kind:** architecture paper  
**Documents:** `docs/architecture.md` (roll-up), `docs/engine/architecture.md`, `docs/product/architecture.md`

## Body

What is built: modules, how they depend, and pointers to the narrower papers or specs. The roll-up stays short and points at the other two.

## Must not

Accumulate a Step-by-Step history. That history is the changelog and the Step records.
