# F-043 — Ridge accretion (diverge fill)

**Goal:** G-009  
**Status:** `done`

## Job (approved)

When plates SEPARATE, gaps are filled by new crust that extends those two plates only (Earth-like ridge accretion). Full cover stays — no unowned cells. Remove global nearest-site / nearest-owner refill. No poles/slivers/UI. No engine edits.

## Decisions (approved)

| Topic | Choice |
|-------|--------|
